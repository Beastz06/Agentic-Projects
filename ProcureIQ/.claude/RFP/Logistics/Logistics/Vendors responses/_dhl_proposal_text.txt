Comprehensive Evaluation
7DHL Supply Chain Proposal | L'Occitane | December 2022
• We evaluated 5 unit pick technologies, for the FY2025 volumes
• We focused on balancing space, throughput, labor costs and capital investment
• Key selection criteria prioritized the following:
i. Flexible to profile changes and volume by brand
ii. Scalable to support steady state and peak volume
iii. Low capital investment
iv. Go-Live by mid 2024
• Assisted Picking Robots (Locus) best fit these requirements:
• Shorter implementation timeline
• Scalable and flexible to volume changes
• Low capital and quick ROI
• Reduce training time for new employees, especially during peak season
• Long term solution to be further evaluated based on network strategy
• Understanding the purpose and timeline for the West DC

Labor Strategy
DHL Supply Chain Proposal | L'Occitane | December 2022 41
In the field of contract logistics, a motivated and well-trained workforce is critical to offering L'Occitane an optimal solution. 
Therefore, we take a proactive and multi-layered approach to both attracting and retaining DHL employee's vs relying on temp 
labor. The shift from temp labor to full-time employees is proving successful, as shown by this renewed certification from the 
Top Employers Institute. 
Specifically in the US, our top-performing areas include workforce planning, performance management, Certified Supply Chain 
Specialist (CSCS) program, online learning and development offerings, succession planning, and college recruiting 
processes. These all add up to outstanding opportunities for every member of our organization and ensure our continued success and 
market leadership.

are largely complex multi-channel operations.

50th and 75th %tile of the starting wage rate.

Disaster Recovery Solution  
Deployed manually in the case of an adverse event, DSC’s disaster recovery solution leverages virtual technology, providing
production application and database switchover between the Ashburn and Mechanicsburg datacenters. This enables DHL to
provide greatly improved SLAs for disaster recovery:
✓ In the event that an adverse event occurs, DHL Supply Chain will replicate all tiers of Application data between Ashburn
and Mechanicsburg, enabling a “warm” standby
✓ According to the RPO, these replications cannot fall more than 30 minutes behind—in reality, ~one minute is the longest
duration in between replications
4 Hour Recovery Time Objective (RTO)
The targeted duration of time within which normal 
operations must be restored after a disaster or 
failure
30 Minutes of Recovery Point Objective (RPO)
The maximum targeted length of time in which 
data can be lost due to a failure or disaster

Business Continuity 
DHL Supply Chain Proposal | L'Occitane | December 2022 66
DHL Supply Chain has developed a comprehensive Business Continuity Management Strategy that all sites utilize for each of their 
respective customers: organized, supported and driven by the BCM 10 Step Methodology program.  All sites are expected to havethe 
architecture in place for an effective business continuity plan published and available on or before Day 1 of Go Live.
A summary of the BCM 10 Step Methodology is outlined below:
Step 1 BCM Policy
The site, in partnership with L'Occitane, will develop a policy that can be 
clearly communicated to all key stakeholders.
Step 2  Provision of Resource
An individual (GM) with the relevant seniority and authority will lead the 
BCM implementation at the site along with the guidance of the Business 
Continuity management team.
Step 3 Incident Management Team
A team will be established, comprised of a primary and deputy member 
with expert knowledge of each site function (Ops, Security, IT, HR).  In the 
event of an incident, a representative of each function will be made 
available to make business critical decisions
Step 4 Business Impact Analysis 
Step 5 Operational Risk Assessment
Step 6 Determining Mitigation Measures
Step 7  Determining BCM Strategy
Step 8 Business Continuity Plans (BCP’s)
Step 9 Maintaining and Reviewing 
Step 10 Testing and Exercising
